Activation and distribution strategy for PuREBEL, a new-age F&B nutrition brand.

The Taste Of Choosing Better

PuREBEL is a new-age F&B brand built around the idea of a "delicious smart choice," fitting into everyday drinking moments, whether it's a milkshake, smoothie, or cup of tea. It's made for students and young professionals, and trusted by mothers choosing better options for their kids. The brief: make "choosing better" feel less like a compromise and more like something people actually want.

The QR Landing Page

A mobile landing page built for the activation touchpoints, scanned on site to rate a sip and unlock the brand experience in the moment.

Strategy Before Style

This project pushed me further upstream than most of my design work. Before any visual identity, I had to understand the competitive landscape and find a defensible position worth designing for. Translating that positioning into physical, testable activation ideas kept the strategy honest and grounded.
