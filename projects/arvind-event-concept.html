A full gala event concept designed end to end for Arvind Fashions.

Soaring Threads, Self Actualization

For Arvind Fashions' annual Gala, the brief called for a theme that celebrated every employee's individual contribution to something greater: the idea that no single thread makes the fabric, but together they weave a company's greatness. I conceptualized the full event, from theme and spatial design through to entertainment and talent booking, around the idea of Ascent, upward motion, self actualization, and limitless becoming.

"We weave mountains to reach our limitless potential. Our Gala Event celebrates every employee creating something greater than any one thread could alone."

Spaces That Tell the Story

Every touchpoint of the venue carried the Ascent motif: mountains, soaring birds, and threads rendered across entrances, bars, and installations.

A Night of Entertainment

Hosts and Emcees

A lineup of RJ emcees paired with celebrity hosts Karan Wahi and Ritvik Dhanjani to anchor the evening's flow.

Live Performances

Drum jam and boomwhacker orchestras, aerial silk and fire dance, a Tron LED act, laser performance, and an MJ5 tribute act.

Music

Celebrity DJ sets from DJ Chetas and Nikhil Chinapa, alongside live bands Lagori, Barfi, Swaradi, The Nineteen, Mysore Xpress, and Savaari.

Designing an Evening, Start to Finish

Ascent was a chance to design an entire guest experience as a single cohesive narrative: theme, space, activity, and performance all answering to one idea of upward momentum. Carrying one concept consistently across an archway, a bar, a dozen craft stations, and a full entertainment bill was the real exercise in event design.
