"Iconic You," a concept award design visualized for Apple's internal iFest 2026 celebration.

Brief: ICONIC YOU

The Thought

From the first Mac to everything that followed, Apple has created products and moments that have become iconic, things that have changed the world.

But behind every icon is a person who dared to think differently and make it happen.

For iFest 2026, we want to put the spotlight back on Apple's people, the individuals who make Apple what it is.

Every employee is the MAC: the Main Apple Character.

Iconic You is about making every employee feel like the icon at the centre of the Apple story.

Visual Direction

I explore the idea of reflection using elements that allow people to see themselves as the icon. Think mirrors, screens, light, reflections, silhouettes, and other visual devices that put the employee at the centre of the experience.

This thought has been interpreted in my own creative style to build a visual language around it.

Material Over Message

Designing for a brand as visually disciplined as Apple meant restraint was the whole brief: one silhouette, one material story, no extra ornamentation. The challenge was making a familiar mark feel new again simply through form and light.
