About

Human Computer Generated Art — an experimentation in TouchDesigner using StreamDiffusion AI. This small exploration focused on generative visuals produced by combining procedural TouchDesigner networks with AI-driven diffusion pipelines, producing cinematic, evolving textures and abstract compositions intended for projection and installation work.

What I Learned

Integrated AI-driven diffusion with real-time TouchDesigner pipelines, optimised networks for reliable live rendering, and composed procedural systems for projection-mapped installations.
